THE HONEST PART

The Supreme Court struck down the IEEPA tariffs on 20 February 2026, which cut average tariffs by about 4.8 points. The Dallas Fed found the Hormuz shipping-cost increase completely offsets it, putting the net tariff effect through 2026 close to zero. So the headline spike is the war, and the tariffs are the slow creep in the core. Both are consequences of decisions taken after January 2025 — but they are not the same number, and we will not add them together.
