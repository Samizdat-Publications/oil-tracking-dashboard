WHAT THIS SHOWS

The war started on 28 February. Oil went from $57 to $114 in five weeks — a 99% jump. It fell all the way back to $69.74 when a ceasefire held in June, below where it started, then rose again to $84 when strikes resumed in July.

Inflation does not switch off on the day of a ceasefire and back on three weeks later. Neither do tariffs. That round trip is why this is an energy shock and not inherited inflation.
