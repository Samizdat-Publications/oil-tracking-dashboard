Method
Event study on pre-classified war events with exact binomial test. The four large pre-registered events (strike, 7 Apr ceasefire, 18 Jun ceasefire, 8 Jul resumption) all moved price in the predicted direction. The 1 Sep event was added after the fact and is labelled so.

They say

Gold is down 20% from its 28 Jan 2026 record of $5,589. The broad dollar index is up since the war began (117.5 → 118.7). The Fed (FEDS Note, 3 Sep 2026) calls the gold-over-Treasuries headline "misleading": private ETF demand drove the price, and foreign officials still bought about $200bn of Treasuries net since 2022. The ECB: the reserve-share crossover is "mainly valuation."

We say

All of that is true and none of it touches the chart above. The Fed's own table counts ounces at a price that has not changed since 1973. Valuation cannot move that row. The ounces left every month for ten months, none arrived, and the central banks that give a reason give the same one the Dutch did: geopolitical unrest. This is not a dollar collapse. It is where the world now chooses to keep its gold.

What would prove this wrong
A month in which the earmarked row rises. There has not been one. Or a Fed statement that the row moved for a reason other than custody transfers; none has been made.
Not claimed
That "countries are pulling all their gold out of America" (159 t of 5,919). That the dollar is collapsing (it is up since February). That Germany has moved anything. Gold price figures are TIER 2 and appear only in the counter-argument.
